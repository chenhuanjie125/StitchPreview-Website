Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

StitchPreview is designed to let you inspect embroidery files locally on your Mac. Privacy is part of the product, not an afterthought.

What the app does

When you ask Finder or Quick Look to preview a supported embroidery file, StitchPreview reads that requested file locally to generate its thumbnail or preview. It is a read-only tool: it does not modify the original file.

Data we collect

The app requires no account or analytics and does not upload embroidery files, file contents, filenames, paths, or stitch data. It does not sell or share design data. Information you choose to include in a support email is handled separately to answer your request.

Local settings and derived files

The app stores local display preferences and may retain derived thumbnail-cache images on your Mac. You can clear the derived cache in the app. It does not retain source files, filenames, paths, or stitch arrays in that cache. If enabled, an anonymous local health receipt records only the extension component, result, time, duration, policy version, and irreversible fingerprint. These local records are not sent to us.

This website

This static website has no contact form, advertising tracker, analytics script, or cookies set by us. The language switch saves your English or Chinese choice in browser local storage on your device. If you email support, your message is handled by your email provider and ours to respond to your request.

Support and voluntary samples

Files are never attached automatically. Support first asks for a description, app and macOS versions, file format, and the visible error. If a diagnostic sample is needed, we will discuss your authority to share it, its local read-only use, and when it will be deleted before you send anything. Do not send customer, purchased, confidential, encrypted, or access-controlled designs. See the support page.

Children

StitchPreview is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do. Questions about this policy can be sent to support@showleafy.com.
